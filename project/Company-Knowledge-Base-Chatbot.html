Company Knowledge Base Chatbot

企業知識對話助理

In many companies, information is scattered across Word, PDF, Excel, and different internal systems. When employees need to look up past records, they often have to search file by file. It takes time, and important details can easily be missed.

This project explores using AI and vector databases to build a conversational knowledge platform. Instead of manually searching, employees can simply ask questions in natural language and get instant answers from an integrated company database.

In the demo for the estimating department, a user can type: "What was the quote for Project X in May last year?" The system retrieves the record and shows the full details in just a few seconds. A process that normally takes 10–15 minutes can now be done almost instantly.

The value goes beyond speed:

  • Reduce knowledge gaps — New employees can quickly learn from past cases, while senior staff spend less time repeating answers.
  • Centralize information — Documents from different formats are stored in one place, reducing version conflicts.
  • Flexible use cases — From estimate records to onboarding guides, internal tech Q&A, or customer service FAQs.

Most importantly, this chatbot shows the potential of having an internal AI assistant. It shifts the focus from searching files to making decisions and creating value. Over time, it can evolve into a smart knowledge platform that supports collaboration and efficiency across the organization.

Technologies Used: Python, OpenAI API, Vector Indexing, SQL Database, n8n, JavaScript, HTML, CSS
Year: 2025

在企業裡，資訊往往散落在不同文件與系統中：Word、PDF、Excel、甚至共享伺服器。當員工需要查詢歷史紀錄時，必須一份份翻找，不僅耗時，也常遺漏關鍵細節。

這個專案嘗試以 AI 與向量資料庫 建立對話式知識平台。透過自然語言提問，員工能即時調閱整合後的公司資訊，而不再依賴傳統的人工搜尋。

在估價部門的 Demo 中，使用者只需輸入：「去年五月某專案的報價是多少？」系統就能自動檢索並回覆完整金額與細節。原本需要 10–15 分鐘的人工作業，如今只要數秒完成。

專案價值不僅在於時間效率：

  • 降低知識斷層 —— 新員工可快速熟悉流程與案例，資深員工也減少重複解答。
  • 資訊集中 —— 不同格式文件統一整合，避免版本不一致。
  • 彈性應用 —— 除了估價紀錄，也能用於內部技術問答、客服常見問題。

最重要的是，這個 Chatbot 展現了 企業內部 AI 助手的可能性。它讓人專注於分析與決策，而不是浪費在搜尋與整理文件。未來，這樣的系統有潛力演進為企業的「智慧知識平台」，成為組織協作與成長的基礎。

使用技術： Python、OpenAI API、向量索引、SQL 資料庫、n8n、JavaScript、HTML、CSS
年份： 2025
