# esterlo.com — Ester Lo

Personal website and portfolio of **Ester Lo** — AI automation and advanced manufacturing. I build AI and automation systems for manufacturing, architecture, and construction companies, designed around how the business actually runs.

**Live site**: [www.esterlo.com](https://www.esterlo.com/) (GitHub Pages, custom domain)

## What's here

- `index.html` — main page: positioning, featured work, contact
- `about.html` — background and experience
- `automation.html` + `project/` — AI & automation case studies (all client/employer material is de-identified; screenshots come from rebuilt demonstration systems, not real internal systems)
- `digifab.html` — digital fabrication and computational design work
- `blog.html` + `blog/articles/` — articles on AI automation, manufacturing, and cross-cultural observations (English and Traditional Chinese)

## Tech

Plain HTML/CSS/JS — no frameworks, no build step. EmailJS powers the contact form. Bilingual content (EN/繁中) via `data-lang` toggles.

To preview locally:

```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
